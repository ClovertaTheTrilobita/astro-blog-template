The first thing I noticed when I picked it up was its heft. This is probably the most substantial onigiri I've ever had from Lawson. The flavor, though, felt a little underwhelming for something so hefty.

The tender, thick omelet had a faint buttery aroma and practically melted in my mouth. The pork chop, on the other hand, was just an ordinary pan-fried piece of pork, a little on the dry side, and felt out of place alongside the omelet. It took extra effort to bite into it without pulling the entire pork chop out of the rice ball.

The pork chop's sauce was sweet and savory, which did pair nicely with the omelet.
