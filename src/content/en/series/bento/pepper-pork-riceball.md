I've forgotten the exact details. I was rushing to catch the bus while eating it this morning and tossed the packaging without thinking...

I vaguely remember it weighing around 170 g.

It's basically an onigiri filled with stir-fried pork and peppers. It's gone beyond the bounds of a traditional rice ball. Is it an onigiri, or an express version of a pork-and-pepper rice bowl? A question worth pondering.

It tasted exactly like the stir-fried pork and peppers I get at the cafeteria, but surprisingly, refrigeration hadn't made the meat unpleasant or thrown off the flavor. Points for that.

Still, it tastes exactly like you'd imagine. Stir-fried pork and peppers, and rice.
