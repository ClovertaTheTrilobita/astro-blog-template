Awful!

The worst onigiri I've ever had from Lawson!

The whole thing tasted of salt. They were pretty generous with the pork, but it was just salty, with no aroma at all.

The greasy, tough shreds of pork left a dry, salty taste in my mouth. I had no appetite for hours afterward 🤮.

The rice tasted like it had been sitting in an insulated lunch container for a full day and night. It tasted off in a way I can barely describe. One bite and I thought the onigiri was days old, but it had only been made six hours earlier.
